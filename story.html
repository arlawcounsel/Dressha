Chapter 01 · The roots

Before the white coat,
there was curiosity.

A family full of mathematics. A child drawn to biology. Colour pencils that would one day sit beside dental instruments. The details matter.

01

A different kind of curiosity

Her parents majored in mathematics, but Dreesha found herself drawn to biology and the living world. She wanted to understand how organisms worked, how the body functioned, and what made life tick.

02

The people who backed the dream

She describes herself as the first doctor in her family. Her father's financial support and her mother's encouragement helped make that path possible.

“Ne nalla solli thara, pesama teacher airu!”A mother's loving observation

Her mother's words reflected something she had noticed early: Dreesha enjoyed explaining things and helping others understand.

03

Colour pencils to dental instruments

As a child, she loved colour pencils. Later, dental instruments became part of her world. Dentistry asks for precision, a creative eye, and patience with small details.

04

A profession with a human outcome

Seeing a patient smile confidently helped dentistry become more than a set of techniques. It became a way to help someone feel better about themselves.
