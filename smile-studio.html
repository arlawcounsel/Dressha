How to play

Little circles.
Every corner.

Press start, then move your mouse or finger over the golden spots. The brush follows your movement and each spot disappears when you pass close enough.

  • Move slowly and cover the whole tooth.
  • Keep going until all spots are gone.
  • Try again to beat your time.
A tiny dental note
This is a playful mini-game, not medical advice. Real brushing should be gentle and thorough, with fluoride toothpaste.
